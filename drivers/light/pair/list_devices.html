Pairing takes a bulb away from its current app

A Bluetooth mesh bulb belongs to one network at a time. Pairing it here removes it from the network it is in now — the manufacturer's app will lose it, and the only way back is a factory reset of the bulb.

Only unpaired or factory-reset bulbs appear below. This app is experimental and is tested with SIG Mesh lighting only.
